# Scene 0.5.0

- New tool: bb. Scene writes a bb theme and selects it, and bb shows it in every open window.
- Settings → General can hide Scene from the Dock and the app switcher. Turn off the menu bar icon too, and Scene runs in the background with its shortcuts. Open Scene from Finder or Spotlight to bring its window back.
- Undo puts back every app the undone theme changed. Before, an app added to Scene after the earlier theme kept the undone theme.
